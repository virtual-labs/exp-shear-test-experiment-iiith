After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

- To study the behaviour of a material under shear loading.
- To conduct a shear test on a specimen under double shear.
- To determine the ultimate shear load carried by the specimen.
- To calculate the shear strength of the material.
- To understand the difference between single shear and double shear conditions.
- To relate shear failure to practical engineering applications involving mechanical and structural connections.
